Student Partnership

Total Image + AMA UT Tyler

Total Image + AMA UT Tyler is a partnership between Total Image and the American Marketing Association chapter of the University of Texas at Tyler.

Through this partnership, businesses can save up to 50% on marketing, branding, and graphic design services by allowing marketing students to work on their projects.

Student teams collaborate on real client work while being guided and supervised by experienced professionals from Total Image, ensuring quality results and providing valuable hands-on experience for students.

If you'd like to participate and save up to 50%, simply select the Total Image + AMA box when requesting a quote.
